Gökova Bay

Here is the land of profound shades of blue. All mountains lead to the direction of sea here. Instead of dying somewhere else and sleeping in light, live here in light. Coastline, jade green grass, faces of the fish look like mythological figures, the fresh air; you can’t compare the beauty of here with Nice, Dalmatia or Monte-Carlo. The emerald green coast are surrounded by pine and sweetgum trees. You are now in the famous coast, which many civilizations have admired. The famous author Fisherman of Halicarnassus says: You can die peacefully after seeing Rome but once you see Gökova, all you want will be to live here. Natural heritage of a thousand years, you should never pass on the Blue-Voyage in Gökova. The fisherman will be here to welcome you. The colour of the journey is here absolutely blue. Cheese, water, sea biscuits, tobacco and raki. During your Blue-Voyage, one can rest his head best way away from the hustle and bustle of cities. This beautiful coastline has inspired many authors such as Cevat Şakir, Bedri Rahmi, Sebahattin Eyüboğlu und Azra Erhat. Idyma is the name of Archipelago’s sister, a blue roar and transparent eternity. Since the time of Dionysos, blue has remained here. With its white foam and waves, Gökova rises itself on the shoulders of Aegean and Mediterranean Sea. It is our duty now to protect this natural heritage.

Duration of Renting / Boarding & Departure Times

You can rent our boat (Gulet) from Saturday until Friday evening, minimum duration of renting is 1 week. If you would like to rent our boat for shorter or longer period of time or take the trip on a different days, we can arrange available dates for your request.

Every boat trip starts at 10.00. Passengers leave the boat at 18.00 on the last day of the trip.

Under the condition that passengers accept to pay the price difference, you can get on & off the boat at different seaports. The price difference varies according to the distance between the first and last seaport.
